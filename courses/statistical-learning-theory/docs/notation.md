# Notation

Math is kept outside Markdown tables because some Markdown renderers process table cells before the math engine.

**Input or feature space:** $\mathcal{X}$

**Output or label space:** $\mathcal{Y}$

**Random input-output pair:** $(X,Y)\sim P$, where $P$ is the unknown joint distribution.

**Training sample:** $\{(X_{i},Y_{i})\}_{i=1}^{n}$, drawn independently according to $P$.

**Prediction function:** $f:\mathcal{X}\to\mathcal{Y}$

**Predicted output:** $\widehat{y}$

**Loss function:** $\ell(\widehat{y},y)$

**Population risk:** $R(f)$

**Empirical risk:** $\widehat{R}_{n}(f)$

**Conditional class probability:** $\eta(x)=P(Y=1\mid X=x)$

**Bayes decision function:** $f^{\star}$

**Bayes risk:** $R^{\star}$

**Function or hypothesis class:** $\mathcal{F}$

**Feature map:** $\phi$

**Kernel function:** $K$

**Hilbert space or reproducing-kernel Hilbert space:** $\mathcal{H}$, depending on context.

**Inner product in $\mathcal{H}$:** $\langle u,v\rangle_{\mathcal{H}}$

**Norm induced by the inner product:** $\lVert u\rVert_{\mathcal{H}}$

**Slack variable for sample $i$:** $\xi_{i}$

**Soft-margin penalty parameter:** $C$

**Hinge function:** $\Phi(t)=\max\{1-t,0\}$

**Kernel section centered at $x$:** $K(\cdot,x)$

**Finite span generated by kernel sections:** $\mathcal{H}_{0}$

**Regularization parameter:** $\lambda$

**Sum of independent random variables:** $S_{n}=\sum_{i=1}^{n}X_{i}$

**Bounded-difference constant for coordinate $i$:** $c_{i}$

**Empirical probability of a set:** $P_{n}(A)=\frac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^{n}\mathbf{1}_{\{X_{i}\in A\}}$

**Worst empirical-probability deviation over a class:** $g(X^{n})=\sup_{A\in\mathcal{A}}\lvert P_{n}(A)-P(A)\rvert$

**Uniform generalization gap:** $g((X,Y)^{n})=\sup_{f\in\mathcal{F}}(R(f)-\widehat{R}_{n}(f))$

**Independent ghost sample:** $(X',Y')^{n}=\{(X_{i}',Y_{i}')\}_{i=1}^{n}$

**Rademacher variable:** $\sigma_{i}\in\{-1,1\}$ with equal probability for both signs.

**Rademacher complexity:** $\mathfrak{R}_{n}(\mathcal{F})$

**Lipschitz constant of the loss:** $M$

**RKHS norm bound:** $B$, where $\lVert w\rVert_{\mathcal{H}}\leq B$

**Class of subsets:** $\mathcal{C}\subseteq 2^{\mathcal{Z}}$

**Trace of $\mathcal{C}$ on a finite set $S$:** $\{S\cap C:C\in\mathcal{C}\}$

**VC dimension:** $\mathrm{VC}(\mathcal{C})$

**Class of affine half-spaces in $\mathbb{R}^{d}$:** $\mathcal{C}_{d}$

**Linear-regression parameter vector:** $\theta$

**Design matrix:** $X\in\mathbb{R}^{m\times(d+1)}$

**Vector of regression targets:** $y\in\mathbb{R}^{m}$

**Linear-regression objective:** $J(\theta)=\lVert y-X\theta\rVert_{2}^{2}$

**Optimization iterate:** $x^{(k)}$ or $\theta^{(k)}$

**Learning rate:** $\eta$

**Gradient Lipschitz constant:** $L$

**Optimal point of a convex objective:** $x^{\star}$

**Accelerated-gradient extrapolated point:** $y^{(k)}$

**Accelerated-gradient momentum sequence:** $t_{k}$

**Composite objective:** $f(x)=g(x)+h(x)$

**Differentiable convex component:** $g$

**Possibly nondifferentiable convex component:** $h$

**Proximal mapping:** $\mathrm{prox}_{\eta h}(u)$

**Subdifferential of $h$ at $x$:** $\partial h(x)$

**Subgradient:** $v\in\partial h(x)$

**Proximal-gradient iterate:** $x^{(k)}$

**Accelerated proximal-gradient extrapolated point:** $y^{(k)}$

**Finite-sum objective:** $f(x)=\frac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^{n}f_{i}(x)$

**Component loss for sample $i$:** $f_{i}$

**Random sample index at SGD iteration $k$:** $i_{k}$

**Stochastic gradient:** $v_{k}=\nabla f_{i_{k}}(x^{(k-1)})$

**Gradient-noise bound:** $\sigma^{2}$

**Average SGD iterate:** $\bar{x}^{(k)}=\frac{1}{k}\sum_{t=1}^{k}x^{(t)}$

**Hidden-neuron weight in the two-layer network:** $w_{r}\in\mathbb{R}^{d}$

**Fixed output-layer sign:** $a_{r}\in\{-1,1\}$

**Number of hidden neurons:** $m$

**ReLU activation:** $\sigma(z)=\max\{0,z\}$

**Two-layer network:** $f(W,x)=\frac{1}{\sqrt{m}}\sum_{r=1}^{m}a_{r}\sigma(w_{r}^{\top}x)$

**Neural-network empirical objective:** $\Phi(W)=\frac{1}{2n}\sum_{i=1}^{n}(y_{i}-f(W,x_{i}))^{2}$

**Vectorized hidden-layer parameters:** $\mathrm{vec}(W)\in\mathbb{R}^{md}$

**Training-prediction vector at iteration $k$:** $\widehat{y}(k)$

**Activation indicator:** $I_{r,i}(k)=\mathbf{1}_{\{w_{r}(k)^{\top}x_{i}\geq 0\}}$

**Tangent-feature matrix:** $Z(k)$

**Iteration-dependent tangent Gram matrix:** $H(k)=Z(k)^{\top}Z(k)$

**Neural Tangent Kernel Gram matrix:** $K$

**Minimum NTK eigenvalue:** $\lambda_{0}=\lambda_{\min}(K)$

**NTK label-complexity quantity:** $y^{\top}K^{-1}y$

## Conventions

- Binary labels are written as $\{-1,1\}$ when following the SVM and Bayes-classifier derivations.
- Indicator functions are written as $\mathbf{1}_{\{E\}}$.
- Population expectations are written with explicit conditioning or distributions whenever ambiguity is possible.
- Optimal quantities use a braced star, such as $f^{\star}$, $w^{\star}$, and $R^{\star}$.
- Accents always include braces, such as $\widehat{y}$ and $\widehat{\eta}$.
- A few symbols are reused (e.g. $\sigma$ for Rademacher variables, ReLU and noise level); each chapter states its local meaning.
